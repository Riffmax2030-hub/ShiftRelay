Privacy Policy & Workforce Data Protection Notice

Riffmax Technology Limited ("ShiftRelay", "Processor") processes workforce identity, attendance, geofence, digital Staff ID, automated shift reminder, and payroll records strictly on behalf of onboarded Enterprise Employers ("Data Controllers") in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023), NDPR, and GDPR standards.

Document Reference: SR-LEGAL-PRIV-2026.5
Data Protection Officer: dpo@shiftrelayos.com
Support Desk: support@shiftrelayos.com
Encryption Standard: AES-256 at Rest / TLS 1.3 in Transit / Signed Digital IDs
01 // POINT-OF-PUNCH GEOLOCATION

Zero Continuous Background Tracking

ShiftRelay respects worker privacy outside active shift hours. GPS geofence coordinates are captured strictly at the moment a worker presses the Clock-In or Clock-Out button or submits a site-verified task checklist.

  • We never track your live location when you are off-shift or after clocking out.
  • Workers without personal smartphones who clock in via an onboarded colleague's Peer Staff ID Scanner have zero personal mobile device telemetry collected.
02 // BIOMETRIC, PASSKEYS & AUTOMATED SHIFT TELEMETRY

WebAuthn Passkeys, Attendance Exceptions & Shift Reminders

When using biometric verification (TouchID, FaceID, Android BiometricPrompt, facial verification capture, or NFC/QR Staff ID authentication), device passkey templates remain protected inside hardware Secure Enclave storage while ShiftRelay stores signed verification proofs and attendance audit trails strictly within your employer tenant.

  • ShiftRelay evaluates scheduled shift windows against punch records to deliver automated pre-shift reminders, overdue clock-out alerts, and post-grace supervisor escalations—eliminating manual gate muster calls.
  • Every late arrival, early departure, missed clock-in/out exception, worker explanation, and supervisor pardon or adjustment decision carries an immutable audit note visible to the worker.
  • ShiftRelay Digital Staff ID Cards carry an encrypted organizational verification token rather than plaintext personal financial data.
03 // STATUTORY PAYROLL & IDENTITY RECORDS

Why We Process BVN/NIN, PenCom & Tax IDs

To compute accurate monthly payroll and statutory schedules for your employer, ShiftRelay processes verified employment data including:

  • Full legal name, employee code, verified work/personal email, and role assignment.
  • Pension RSA PIN (PenCom), Tax Identification Number (PAYE), NHF number, and designated salary bank account details.
  • Professional CV credentials, education history, and safety certifications uploaded to your Worker Profile.
04 // WORKER RECTIFICATION RIGHTS

Right to Inspect & Rectify via Official Receipts

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), every worker has the right to request correction of inaccurate attendance, exception, identity, or payroll records.

  • Use the in-app Timesheet Exceptions review flow or Support Desk & Rectification Receipt generator (`RECT-*`) to submit explanations or lodge correction requests.
  • Every receipt is delivered to your email (`support@shiftrelayos.com`) and routed to verified Operations Support staff with a complete audit trail.
  • Marketing & SMS/WhatsApp Preferences: Workers and newsletter subscribers can opt out anytime via one-click `/unsubscribe` links or in-app NDPA notification controls without affecting essential employment records.
Questions about Data Sovereignty, NDPA Audits, or Sub-processors? Reach our Data Protection Officer at dpo@shiftrelayos.com or support@shiftrelayos.com
