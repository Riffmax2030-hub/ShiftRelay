Terms of Service & Workplace Confidentiality

Workplace Confidentiality is an Absolute Requirement:

ShiftRelay connects frontline teams and empowers shift workers. All users are strictly prohibited from publishing or leaking confidential employer, client, or patient data on any public area of the platform (including the Shift Lounge, media uploads, and public comments).

1. Prohibited Disclosures on Public Feeds

  • Healthcare (HIPAA & GDPR): Patient names, vital charts, room numbers, diagnoses, or clinical photography.
  • Logistics & Warehouse: Internal inventory manifests, client shipping volumes, gate security PINs, or facility access codes.
  • Hospitality & Food Service: Proprietary culinary formulas/recipes, internal POS revenues, or guest booking lists.
  • Manufacturing & Engineering: Proprietary blueprints, equipment downtime logs, and trade secrets.

2. Private Organisation Relays vs. Public Shift Lounge

Internal Organisation Portal: Shift handovers, daily task checklists, supervisor reviews, and internal team chats are encrypted and strictly accessible only to verified workers of your specific organization code.

Public Shift Lounge: Dedicated solely to general career milestones, non-proprietary shift tips, personal craft recognition, and positive frontline community. Leaking internal data will result in immediate ban, revocation of verified status, and employer notification.
