## 1. Executive Summary

As **ShiftRelay Technologies Limited** transitions from initial supported launch validation (4 paid industrial enterprise clients, 358 frontline workers, ₦2.003M MRR, 90.4% SaaS gross margin) to institutional scaling across the Nigerian manufacturing and logistics sectors, executive leadership must scale ahead of headcount.

Hiring full-time, C-suite executives at this stage would create unnecessary payroll burn. Utilizing the **Connectd** platform, ShiftRelay is engaging experienced **Fractional Leadership** to provide strategic governance, enterprise B2B sales acceleration, and regulatory oversight without the full-time financial commitment.

### Executive Search Scope:
1. **Fractional Chief Commercial Officer (CCO) / B2B Enterprise Growth Lead:** To scale our enterprise pipeline from 4 accounts to 25 manufacturing/logistics hubs across Lagos, Ogun, and Rivers states.
2. **Fractional Chief Financial Officer (CFO) / Strategic Capital Advisor:** To structure our statutory financial reporting, model working capital for multi-gate tablet rollouts, and lead our **₦35,000,000 (~$22,000 USD) Pre-Seed funding round**.
3. **Advisory Board Member (Industrial HR & Labour Governance):** Former FMCG HR Director to guide enterprise procurement navigation, union alignment, and PenCom/FIRS institutional compliance.

## 3. Targeted Fractional Leadership Roles & Role Specifications

### 3.1 Role 1: Fractional Chief Commercial Officer (CCO)
* **Objective:** Formalize enterprise B2B sales playbooks, manage the 545-worker referral pipeline (Honeywell Flour Mills, Freezlink Cold Stores, Tincan Port Gate Team), and negotiate multi-site annual contracts with industrial conglomerates.
* **Commitment:** 1 to 2 days per week (approx. 30–40 hours per month).
* **Ideal Candidate Profile:**
  * 10+ years B2B enterprise software or industrial services sales experience in West Africa.
  * Direct relationships with Operations Directors and C-suite leaders across the Manufacturers Association of Nigeria (MAN) and Nigerian Employers' Consultative Association (NECA).
  * Proven track record closing ₦15M–₦50M ACV enterprise contracts.
* **Key Deliverables (First 90 Days):**
  * Convert current warm referral pipeline into at least 3 signed enterprise contracts.
  * Establish an inbound B2B marketing engine and outbound SDR cadence for Ikeja and industrial Lagos.
  * Optimize contract pricing models for large manufacturing plants exceeding 500 workers.

---

### 3.2 Role 2: Fractional Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
* **Objective:** Oversee statutory audit compliance, implement SaaS metric reporting (CAC, LTV, Net Retention, Payback Period), manage cash runway, and drive investor readiness for institutional angel/pre-seed syndicates.
* **Commitment:** 1 day per week (approx. 16–20 hours per month).
* **Ideal Candidate Profile:**
  * Chartered Accountant (ICAN / ACCA / CFA) with previous venture-backed tech startup experience.
  * Familiarity with cross-border holding company structures (e.g., Nigeria OpCo / Delaware or UK TopCo).
  * Deep knowledge of Nigerian tax laws (PITA, CITA, VAT, WHT) and Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) regulatory boundaries.
* **Key Deliverables (First 90 Days):**
  * Deliver institutional-grade financial projections and data-room ready historical ledgers.
  * Package the ₦35M pre-seed pitch deck, cap table model, and SAFE notes for investor circulation.
  * Set up automated revenue recognition and monthly financial dashboards.

---

### 3.3 Role 3: Advisory Board Member (Frontline Labour & Industrial Relations)
* **Objective:** Provide high-level governance, assist in navigating trade union sensitivities (e.g., National Union of Food, Beverage and Tobacco Employees - NUFBTE), and position ShiftRelay as an empowering, transparent tool for frontline workers rather than a punitive surveillance measure.
* **Commitment:** Quarterly board meeting + 2 hours monthly ad-hoc strategic consultation.
* **Ideal Profile:** Former HR Director or VP of People from an enterprise manufacturer (e.g., Nigerian Breweries, Unilever, Nestlé Nigeria, or Flour Mills of Nigeria).
