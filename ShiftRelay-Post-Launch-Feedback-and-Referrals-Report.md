## 4. Employer & Worker Feedback Synthesis

To ensure continuous improvement, structured feedback interviews were conducted with three stakeholder groups: **Frontline Workers**, **Shift Supervisors**, and **Finance/HR Directors**.

### 4.1 Frontline Worker Feedback
* **Key Positive:** Workers widely appreciated the transparent digital payslip. Knowing their exact PITA tax and PenCom pension balance on the 25th of the month eliminated rumors of arbitrary payroll cuts.
* **Constructive Feedback:** Workers noted that under heavy factory vibration or wet hands (cold storage/bottling), the touchscreen button for "Clock Out" was occasionally difficult to trigger on entry-level Android devices.
* **Quote:** *"Before ShiftRelay, if supervisor vex with you, your overtime fit disappear from book. Now, once I scan gate QR code, the time enter system sharp sharp. Nobody fit cut my money."* — Emeka Okafor, Senior Mill Operator, Flour Mills.

### 4.2 Shift Supervisor Feedback
* **Key Positive:** Complete elimination of manual morning roll calls. Supervisors save 30 to 45 minutes at every shift handover.
* **Constructive Feedback:** Supervisors requested a single-button "Approve All Verified Punches" rather than clicking individual punch authorizations for minor on-site delays under 5 minutes.

### 4.3 Finance & HR Director Feedback
* **Key Positive:** Month-end payroll compilation time dropped from **5 business days to 12 minutes**. Reconciled bank CSV files upload seamlessly into commercial banking portals (Access Bank, Zenith Bank, GTBank).
* **Constructive Feedback:** Requested an automated PenCom schedule export formatted specifically for individual Pension Fund Administrator (PFA) upload portals (Stanbic IBTC, ARM, Leadway).
