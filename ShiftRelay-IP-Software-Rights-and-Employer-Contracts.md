# ShiftRelay Intellectual Property, Software IP Assignment & Master Services Agreement
## Phase: Legal Hardening | Milestone: Trademark Clearance, IP Assignment & Enterprise Terms

---

### Executive Objective
This legal specification establishes the formal intellectual property (IP) protection, software ownership chain of title, open-source licensing audit, and commercial contract framework for **ShiftRelay Technologies Limited**. It documents a trademark clearance search under the Nigerian Trademarks Act (Class 9 & Class 42), formalizes a founder-to-company Software IP Assignment Agreement, audits runtime code licenses, and delivers a comprehensive enterprise **Master Services Agreement (MSA) and Terms of Service** covering setup fees, subscription billing, multi-tier payroll approval, data protection (NDPA 2023), SLA support tiers, mutual liability limits, and contract cancellation protocols.

## 4. Enterprise Master Services Agreement (MSA) & Employer Terms

The following binding contractual terms govern all corporate engagements between ShiftRelay and client manufacturing enterprises:

```
               SHIFTRELAY ENTERPRISE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA)
                               [TERMS OF SERVICE]

Between: SHIFTRELAY TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED ("Provider")
And:     [ENTERPRISE CLIENT NAME PLC / LTD] ("Client")

1. SUBSCRIPTION FEES & PAYMENT TERMS
1.1 Setup & Blueprinting Fee: The Client shall pay the one-time Onboarding & Gate
    Blueprinting Fee (as designated in the Order Form) prior to the deployment of
    physical muster QR posters and employee CSV roster intake.
1.2 Monthly Subscription: Recurring fees comprise the Base Platform Fee plus the
    Per-Employee-Per-Month (PEPM) rate based on active headcount.
1.3 Invoicing & Net Terms: Invoices are rendered on the 1st of each month with Net 15
    day payment terms. Invoices include statutory Value Added Tax (VAT 7.5%). Client
    is entitled to deduct 5% Withholding Tax (WHT) subject to providing valid FIRS
    WHT Credit Notes within 30 days.

2. PAYROLL SIGN-OFF & ALLOCATION OF RESPONSIBILITY
2.1 Verification Role: ShiftRelay operates strictly as an authoritative computation,
    muster verification, and scheduling platform.
2.2 Final Approval Mandate: The Client's authorized finance and HR officers maintain
    exclusive legal responsibility for reviewing timesheets, approving overtime, and
    executing the "Push to Finance Desk" workflow.
2.3 Bank Disbursement Execution: ShiftRelay does not handle client deposit balances or
    execute banking wires directly. The Client remains solely responsible for
    uploading exported bank CSV schedules into its corporate banking clearing switch.

3. DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY GOVERNANCE
3.1 NDPA 2023 Compliance: Both parties commit to strict adherence to the Nigeria Data
    Protection Act (NDPA 2023). Provider acts as a Data Processor of Major Importance;
    Client acts as Data Controller.
3.2 Security Safeguards: Provider shall maintain TLS 1.3 in-transit and AES-256
    at-rest encryption. Zero continuous mobile GPS tracking is conducted.
3.3 Audit Trail Retention: Client employee payroll records and muster scans shall be
    retained for six (6) years in accordance with federal tax statutes.

4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & SUPPORT TIERS
4.1 Uptime Guarantee: Provider guarantees 99.8% monthly cloud system availability,
    excluding scheduled maintenance during off-peak weekend hours (01:00-04:00 WAT).
4.2 Support Tiers:
    - Critical (System Outage): 1-Hour Response SLA; 24/7 emergency dispatch.
    - Standard (Configuration/Payroll Queries): 4-Hour Response SLA during business hours.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
5.1 Mutual Direct Liability Cap: Except for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or
    breach of Section 3 (Data Protection), each party's aggregate liability under this
    Agreement shall be strictly capped at the total subscription fees paid by Client
    to Provider in the twelve (12) months preceding the incident.
5.2 Consequential Damages: Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or
    consequential loss of industrial production or prospective factory profits.

6. TERM, CANCELLATION & OFFBOARDING
6.1 Initial Term: Initial term shall be twelve (12) months from the Go-Live Date,
    auto-renewing for successive 12-month periods unless terminated.
6.2 Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing
    sixty (60) days' prior written notice after the expiration of the Initial Term.
6.3 Termination for Cause: Immediate termination permitted upon material breach remaining
    uncured for thirty (30) days following formal written notice.
6.4 Data Offboarding: Upon termination, Provider shall provide a comprehensive CSV/SQL
    export of all historical attendance, timesheets, and payslips within fourteen (14)
    days, followed by secure cryptographic data deletion from active server partitions.

7. GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any dispute
arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved through binding
arbitration under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023, conducted in Lagos, Nigeria.
```
