Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Executed between the Subscribed Employer (Data Controller) and Riffmax Technology Limited operating ShiftRelay OS (Data Processor). Aligned with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) and Nigerian Labour Act.

01 // ROLES & LAWFUL BASIS

Controller vs. Processor Responsibilities

  • Data Controller: The onboarded Employer determines lawful bases for processing employee attendance, shift handovers, payroll inputs, PenCom RSA PINs, and NUBAN disbursement details.
  • Data Processor: Riffmax Technology Limited processes personal data strictly on documented Controller instructions, never monetizes employee data, and never pools personnel across tenants.
  • Governing Frameworks: NDPA 2023, Nigerian Labour Act (Cap L1 LFN 2004), PITA 2011 Finance Acts, and PenCom PRA 2014.
02 // TECHNICAL & ORGANISATIONAL CONTROLS

Encryption, Tenant Isolation & Security Posture

ShiftRelay OS is ISO/IEC 27001 Security Controls Aligned across application and storage tiers:

  • In-Transit & At-Rest Encryption: TLS 1.3 across all edge and API channels; AES-256 encryption on PostgreSQL volumes and document vaults.
  • Strict Tenant Isolation: Every query scopes by authenticated organisation_id with role-based access control (RBAC) across Worker, Supervisor, Finance/HR, and Platform Admin surfaces.
  • Point-of-Punch GPS Minimization: Geofence validation runs strictly during clock-in and clock-out events — zero background location collection off-shift.
03 // INCIDENT SLA & RETENTION

72-Hour Breach Notification & Termination Deletion

  • 72-Hour Notification SLA: Processor notifies Controller without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirming any security incident affecting Customer personal data, supporting NDPC reporting duties.
  • Data Return & Purge: Upon contract termination, Customer exports payroll ledgers, attendance exceptions, and audit logs in CSV/HTML format; active tenant operational records are purged or anonymized within 30 days, subject to 6-year Nigerian statutory tax retention rules where required by law.
