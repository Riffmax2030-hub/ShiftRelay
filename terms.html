CLAUSE 01 // ENTERPRISE PROVISIONING

Scope of Platform & Concierge B2B Onboarding

ShiftRelay provides an enterprise-grade B2B workforce operations, attendance verification, shift handover, and statutory payroll automation platform. Access to the ShiftRelay Application is strictly provisioned through authorized Enterprise Tenant Agreements. Public self-registration on the corporate website (shiftrelay.com) is intentionally disabled to maintain strict organizational tenant isolation.

  • Tenant Isolation: Each onboarded enterprise receives a unique Organization Code, dedicated geofence perimeter coordinates, and isolated roster ledgers.
  • Worker Provisioning: Frontline staff, shift supervisors, and facility managers are provisioned exclusively via verified Employer CSV Roster imports or authorized HR Admin onboarding workflows.
CLAUSE 02 // ACCESS CONTROL & GATE TABLETS

Multi-Role Authentication & 2-Layer Gate Tablet Security

The ShiftRelay application dynamically adapts its interface based on device profile and authenticated role (Frontline Worker, Shift Supervisor, Executive/Owner, or Dedicated Gate Attendance Tablet).

  • 2-Layer Gate Kiosk Lock: Tablets deployed at factory or facility gates require (i) the Company Unique Gate Code and (ii) a real-time 6-digit Verification Code dispatched to the registered Owner/Admin email address.
  • Tamper Prevention: Exiting or logging out of a Gate Kiosk mode requires re-verification by the organization's Owner or Authorized Administrator.
  • Encrypted NFC & Rotating QR: Gate Tablets generate 60-second rotating cryptographic TOTP QR codes and read MIFARE DESFire EV3 encrypted NFC employee badges to prevent buddy-punching or photocopy spoofing.
CLAUSE 03 // ATTENDANCE TELEMETRY

Biometric Water-Pulse Clock-In, GPS Geofencing & Offline Sync

By clocking in via the ShiftRelay Mobile App or Gate Kiosk, Users and Customer Organizations acknowledge that ShiftRelay records cryptographically signed attendance timestamps, GPS perimeter validation at the moment of punch, or NFC/QR physical presence tokens.

  • Actual vs. Scheduled Hours: Attendance logs record actual verified clock-in and clock-out events separately from scheduled shift rosters.
  • Supervisor Dual-Capacity: Shift Supervisors who also execute active floor shifts may record personal clock-in/clock-out attendance alongside their team supervision duties.
CLAUSE 04 // WORKFLOW INTEGRITY

Team-Scoped Shift Swaps, Checklists & ShiftCoin Rewards

Shift swaps and open shift claims are strictly scoped to verified coworkers within the same enterprise tenant and operational unit. Cross-company shift visibility or unauthorized third-party substitution is strictly prohibited.

  • Handover Accountability: Workers must complete assigned safety and operational checklists and submit handover notes prior to shift completion.
  • ShiftCoin Stars (★): Punctuality, verified handovers, and peer coverage award non-transferable ShiftCoin stars redeemable solely within the employer-authorized reward catalog.
CLAUSE 05 // STATUTORY COMPLIANCE

Automated Payroll Computation & Statutory Remittance Schedules

ShiftRelay automates gross-to-net wage calculations based on verified attendance hours, overtime multipliers, and Nigerian statutory deduction schedules including Pension Reform Act (PenCom 8% Employee / 10% Employer), Personal Income Tax (PAYE Finance Act), National Housing Fund (NHF 2.5%), and NSITF (1%).

While ShiftRelay generates audit-ready CSV/PDF schedules and disbursement batches, final treasury release and statutory remittance filing remain the responsibility of the Customer Organization's designated Owner or Finance Officer.

CLAUSE 06 // DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Official Rectification Receipts & Immutable Audit Logs

Workers who identify discrepancies in attendance timestamps, identity records, or wage computations may generate an official Rectification Receipt (`RECT-*`) through the in-app Support Desk. Every Rectification Receipt is stamped with the worker's verified email, employee ID, and timestamp, and routed to the Operations Support & HR Admin queue for audited resolution.
