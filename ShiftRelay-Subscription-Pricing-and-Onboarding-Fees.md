## 2. Granular Inclusions: What Setup & Support Specifically Cover

### 2.1 The One-Time Onboarding Fee Deliverables
Unlike legacy software vendors who charge open-ended consulting fees, ShiftRelay's onboarding fee covers a concrete 48-hour operational go-live package:

1. **Gate Architecture & Physical Muster Engineering:**
   - On-site calibration of physical site boundaries (geofence latitude/longitude + radius calibration).
   - Generation and physical delivery of high-contrast, laminated vector Gate QR Muster posters (Level-H error correction) mounted at factory gates and emergency muster stations.
2. **Employee Roster & Historical Data Migration:**
   - Cleaning, de-duplication, and CSV bulk import of historical employee profiles, shift bands, department assignments, and RSA pension PINs.
   - Generation and delivery of single-use activation tokens via SMS/WhatsApp for worker device setup.
3. **Statutory Payroll Engine Calibration:**
   - Configuration of specific factory overtime multipliers (normal overtime 1.25x–1.5x, rest day / public holiday overtime 2.0x).
   - Validation of state-specific PAYE tax tables (e.g., LIRS Lagos, OGIRS Ogun) and Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA) calculation rules.
4. **On-Site Plant & Supervisor Training:**
   - 2-day hands-on training for Shift Supervisors, Plant Engineers, and Security Gate Commanders.
   - Frontline worker orientation sessions (demonstrating 1-touch WebAuthn biometric login and offline punch caching).

### 2.2 Ongoing Support SLAs Included in Subscription
* **Professional Tier:** 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Monday – Saturday) via dedicated WhatsApp Business helpline and email ticket desk (4-hour response SLA).
* **Enterprise Tier:** 24/7/365 direct telephone hotline for Shift Supervisors, live remote system monitoring, automated monthly statutory rate updates (Finance Act amendments auto-deployed), and a Dedicated Customer Success Manager.
* **Enterprise Plus:** All Enterprise inclusions plus quarterly on-site forensic payroll compliance reviews, custom ERP database connectors (SAP/Oracle batch feeds), and 1-hour critical response SLA.

## 4. Evidence Behind the Offer: Acceptable Prices & Objections

### 4.1 Price Testing Findings
During customer discovery with Nigerian manufacturing and operational personnel:
* **The "Zero Hardware" Discount Advantage:** Traditional biometric fingerprint turnstiles (ZKTeco/Suprema) cost factories ₦2.5M–₦4.5M in upfront hardware, plus ₦500k annual maintenance, with constant failure from greasy/dirty plant hands. Because ShiftRelay utilizes the worker’s own device and laminated vector QR posters, employers viewed the **₦1.5M setup fee as a 60% savings** compared to hardware alternatives.
* **Acceptable Recurring Price Points:**
  - Employers with <100 workers accepted **₦3,000 – ₦4,000 PEPM**.
  - 24/7 continuous process plants (100–500 workers) willingly accepted **₦4,500 – ₦5,500 PEPM**, citing that downtime prevention and statutory penalty protection far outweighed the fee.

### 4.2 Documented Objections & Structured Commercial Resolutions

| Employer Objection | Commercial Reason / Root Cause | ShiftRelay Contractual & Technical Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| *"₦1.5M setup fee is high before we see the system work."* | Budget freeze on unproven software. | **30-Day Paid Sandbox Line Pilot:** Offer a single production line pilot (50 workers) for a flat ₦250,000. On formal contract signing, the ₦250,000 is 100% credited against the onboarding fee. |
