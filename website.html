3-STEP PEER ID + LIVE PASSPORT GUARDRAIL

Zero-Phone Clock-In With Live Selfie vs. HR Passport Match

For workers without a smartphone or with a dead battery, any teammate opens the built-in Peer ID Scanner on the Clock-In page, verifies their live selfie against the worker’s locked HR passport photo inside geofence bounds, and records shift handover acceptance.

GUIDED 3-STEP PEER SCANNER PROTOCOL
  1. Step 1 (ID Verification): Scan colleague’s rotating Digital Staff ID Card token.
  2. Step 2 (Biometric + GPS): Capture live selfie compared against locked HR passport file.
  3. Anti-Proxy Lock: Mismatched faces fail biometric threshold immediately (audit logged).
  4. Step 3 (Handover & Safety): Colleague accepts shift handover & mandatory daily checklist.
  5. Worker closes shift on peer exit scan or automated shift-end policy completion.
