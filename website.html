Questions

Straight answers before you switch

Do we need to buy fingerprint machines or kiosk tablets?

No. Workers clock in from their own phones inside your GPS perimeter, and workers without smartphones simply scan their printed Staff ID QR card on a supervisor’s or colleague’s phone with live facial verification.

Which Nigerian banks and pension portals are supported?

ShiftRelay exports bank-ready disbursement CSVs for GTBank GAPS, Access Online, Zenith Bank, and NIBSS, alongside PFA-grouped PenCom schedules and state-of-residence NTA 2025 PAYE remittances.

Does ShiftRelay hold our payroll cash?

Never. Your funds always stay in your corporate bank account — ShiftRelay verifies the shifts, calculates every tax and deduction accurately, and prepares your ready-to-approve disbursement file.
