FAQ

Common deployment questions.

What if frontline staff don't have smartphones or data?

They can clock in using their printed or digital Staff ID QR card on a supervisor's or teammate's phone via Peer Scan, which verifies their face live against their HR profile.

Does clock-in work in low-signal basements or ports?

Yes. Punches are cryptographically queued offline on the device and automatically sync to the server as soon as connectivity returns—preserving the exact punch timestamp.

Which Nigerian banks and pension portals are supported?

ShiftRelay exports formatted disbursement CSVs compatible with GTBank GAPS, Access Online, Zenith Bank, and NIBSS, alongside PenCom PRA 2014 and PAYE remittance schedules.

How is company and worker data protected?

Every employer runs in a strictly isolated tenant with row-scoped PostgreSQL queries, AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and NDPA 2023 consent controls.
