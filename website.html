PEER ID SCAN & AUTO CLOCK-OUT WORKFLOW

Zero-Phone Attendance with Digital Staff ID Cards

Every onboarded worker receives a Digital Staff ID Card in ShiftRelay. Workers without smartphones simply have an onboarded colleague scan their Staff ID Card once per shift inside the ShiftRelay mobile app—and Auto Clock-Out handles shift completion automatically.

PEER SCAN & AUTO CLOCK-OUT PROTOCOL
  1. Employer onboards company roster and issues signed Digital Staff ID Cards.
  2. On-site colleague opens Peer ID Scanner inside the ShiftRelay mobile app.
  3. Scans worker's Staff ID Card (or verifies Employee ID) to clock in once per shift.
  4. Supervisor list view updates live with peer-verified timestamp and audit badge.
  5. Auto Clock-Out closes open shifts automatically when the shift window completes.
