ACCEPTABLE USE & REGULATORY BOUNDARY · VERSION 2026.11-AUP

Acceptable Use Policy & CBN Zero-Lending Boundary

Protects the cryptographic integrity of frontline attendance ledgers and states ShiftRelay OS's strict Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) regulatory boundary.

01 // PROHIBITED ATTENDANCE MANIPULATION

Anti-Spoofing, Buddy-Punching & Credential Hygiene

  • No GPS Spoofing or Mock Location Injection: Using developer mock-location apps, emulators, or synthetic coordinate spoofing to bypass facility perimeter checks violates this policy and triggers automated security flags.
  • No Proxy / Buddy-Punching: Presenting another person's Digital Staff ID Card or substituting another individual during Peer ID facial capture is strictly prohibited.
  • No Credential Sharing: Supervisors, Finance Officers, and Tenant Admins must never share session passwords, MFA tokens, or WebAuthn passkeys.
02 // CBN ZERO-LENDING REGULATORY BOUNDARY

No Wallets, Earned Wage Access Loans, or Stored Value

  • Software & Ledger Calculation Only: ShiftRelay OS computes gross-to-net Nigerian statutory payroll schedules (PAYE, PenCom, NHF, NSITF) and exports standard bank bulk-upload CSV schedules (GTBank GAPS, AccessPay, Zenith, NIBSS e-BillsPay, Generic CSV).
  • Zero Financial Intermediation: ShiftRelay OS does not custody customer funds, hold Naira digital wallets, issue Earned Wage Access (EWA) loans, charge interest, or execute direct interbank settlement. All salary transfers occur directly between the Employer's commercial bank and employee NUBAN accounts.
