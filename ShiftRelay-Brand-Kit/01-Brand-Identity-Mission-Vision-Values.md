# ShiftRelay Brand Identity, Mission, Vision & Core Values
**Operated by:** Riffmax Technology Limited (`shiftrelayos.com`)  
**Compliance Posture:** NDPA 2023 Data Processor · ISO/IEC 27001 Controls Aligned · Nigeria Tax Act (NTA) 2025 (2026 PAYE) & PenCom PRA 2014 Ready  

---

## 1. Mission
To eliminate ghost-worker leakage, buddy punching, and manual payroll errors across African frontline industries by unifying biometric-verified shift attendance, accountable handovers, and automated statutory payroll into one 48-hour deployable platform.

## 2. Vision
To become the trusted operating system for frontline workforces across Africa—where every shift is verified, every handover is accountable, and every worker's pay, pension, and tax compliance is accurate to the kobo.

## 3. Core Company Values
1. **Zero-Leakage Truth:** Attendance and payroll must be backed by cryptographic proof (geofence, Gate QR, live face/biometric match), never paper guesswork.
2. **Accountable Continuity:** Shifts never end in silence—outgoing supervisors hand over safety, equipment, and clinical/plant state cleanly to incoming teams.
3. **Statutory & Privacy Integrity:** Strict adherence to Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) data minimization, zero-lending CBN boundaries, and automated Finance Act / PenCom / NHF / NSITF compliance.
4. **Zero-Hardware Speed:** Enterprise deployment should take **48 hours**, not 8 weeks of turnstile hardware installation.
5. **Respect for Frontline Dignity:** Workers never pay to clock in or access their earned pay; all platform fees are employer-funded.
