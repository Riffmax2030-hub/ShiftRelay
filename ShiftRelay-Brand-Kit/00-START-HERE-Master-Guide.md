## 2. Final Agreed Pricing (Employer-Paid Only)
*Workers are never charged. Monthly Base + PEPM starts **after** one-time onboarding go-live.*

| Tier | Headcount | Monthly Base | Volume PEPM | One-Time Onboarding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Professional** | Up to 150 | ₦150,000 / mo | **₦4,500 / worker** | **₦750,000** |
| **Enterprise** | Up to 500 | ₦400,000 / mo | **₦3,500 / worker** | **₦1,500,000** |
| **Enterprise Plus** | Up to 1,500 | ₦750,000 / mo | **₦2,500 / worker** | **₦3,000,000** |

- **30-Day Pilot:** **₦250,000** flat (up to 50 workers) — **100% credited to onboarding** upon contract signing.
- **Seasonal Surge (<90 days):** **₦2,500 / worker / mo** (no base tier upgrade).
- **Enterprise Overage:** **₦3,000 / worker / mo**.
