# ShiftRelay Corporate Structure & Legal Incorporation Specification
## Phase: Legal Formation | Milestone: CAC Entity Registration, Share Allotment & PSC Compliance

---

### Executive Objective
This incorporation specification documents the corporate establishment of **ShiftRelay Technologies Limited** under Nigerian company law (**Companies and Allied Matters Act, CAMA 2020**). It provides a rigorous legal comparison of entity structures, confirms the selection of a **Private Company Limited by Shares (Ltd)** to support B2B enterprise procurement and venture equity investment, itemizes statutory formation expenses, outlines the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) filing workflow, details initial share distribution and capitalization (10,000,000 Ordinary Shares), and provides formal compliance documentation for Persons with Significant Control (PSC) beneficial ownership disclosures.

---

## 1. Legal Entity Structure Evaluation

We compared three statutory business forms available under Nigerian company law:

| Entity Structure | CAMA 2020 Statutory Baseline | Enterprise Contracting & Vendor Viability | Venture Capital / Investor Equity Suitability | Liability & Asset Shielding | Final Determination |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Business Name (Sole Proprietorship / Partnership)** | Part B, CAMA 2020 | **REJECTED.** Large enterprises (e.g., Flour Mills, Dangote, BUA) strictly mandate registered corporate limited entities (Ltd) for vendor empanelment and enterprise SaaS billing. | **UNSUITABLE.** Cannot issue equity shares, convertible notes (SAFEs), or employee stock option pools (ESOP). | **ZERO.** Unlimited personal liability; founder personal assets exposed to contract claims. | **DISQUALIFIED** |
| **Private Company Limited by Guarantee (Ltd/Gte)** | Part A, Section 21(1)(b) | **UNSUITABLE.** Non-profit structure restricted to charity, education, or scientific promotion; cannot distribute commercial profits. | **UNSUITABLE.** Cannot issue capital stock or pay dividend returns to equity investors. | Limited by guarantee amount. | **DISQUALIFIED** |
| **Private Company Limited by Shares (Ltd)** | Part A, Section 21(1)(a) | **OPTIMAL.** The gold standard for Nigerian enterprise B2B vendors; satisfies corporate procurement and tax withholding (WHT) mandates. | **PERFECT.** Full legal capacity to issue equity, SAFEs, preference shares, and option pools; recognized by institutional VCs. | **MAXIMUM.** Shareholder liability strictly capped at the nominal value of unpaid shares held. | **CONFIRMED & ADOPTED** |

### Confirmation of Legal Structure:
**ShiftRelay Technologies Limited** is formally established as a **Private Company Limited by Shares** under Part A of CAMA 2020. This structure is required to:
1. Sign multi-million Naira Master Services Agreements (MSAs) with blue-chip manufacturing enterprises.
2. Accept institutional pre-seed venture capital using standard Y Combinator Post-Money SAFEs.
3. Protect founders and employees behind a corporate liability veil.
