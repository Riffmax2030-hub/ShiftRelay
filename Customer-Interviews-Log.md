# ShiftRelay Customer Discovery & Interview Log
## Validation Log: Nigerian Attendance & Payroll Practices

---

### Interview 01
* **Date:** October 30, 2026
* **Channel:** LinkedIn Direct Messaging
* **Interviewee:** Oluwafemi Omonijo
* **Type/Organization:** Nigerian Institutional / Enterprise Organization
* **Current Operational Model:** Fixed Schedule (Non-Shift Corporate / Institutional)

#### Raw Transcript
> **Sherif Akanmu:** Hi Sir, I’m researching how companies manages shift attendance and monthly payroll. Could I ask about your current process for 20 minutes? This isn’t a sales presentation, and I don’t need employee names or salary records.  
> **Oluwafemi Omonijo:** Ok that is not a problem.  
> **Sherif Akanmu:** Alright thank you for your help. Walk me through your last shift change. How did you confirm who arrived, who left, and who covered absences?  
> **Oluwafemi Omonijo:** Sadly we don’t do shift change at my organization.  
> **Sherif Akanmu:** Thanks for your response. I am aware most jobs in Nigeria are not shift based. How are hours recorded? Tell me about the last missing or disputed attendance entry. How was it resolved? What do you use—paper, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, biometric devices, or software? Where do you copy information between tools?  
> **Oluwafemi Omonijo:** We record hours using electronic registers at the door way, we swipe our ID cards at the mornings of entry and at the evening hours of exit. Copying of the information is the duty of the HR department. Asides the electronic registers we also have paper registers at the departmental entry points for signing in and out of the office.  
> **Sherif Akanmu:** Thanks for clarifying. Even without shifts, how does HR use the electronic and paper attendance records? Can you describe the last missing swipe or disagreement between them, how it was resolved, and whether it affected pay?  
> **Oluwafemi Omonijo:** I can’t give much details into that as I am not in that department to fully understand their workflow.  
> **Sherif Akanmu:** Thanks that’s really helpful, and no problem at all. Would you be comfortable introducing me to someone in HR or payroll who handles attendance records? I’d like to understand the process, without requesting confidential employee information.  
> **Oluwafemi Omonijo:** Well there are projects running on AI automation at the institute already.  
> **Sherif Akanmu:** Thanks I appreciate your time and the helpful insight.

---

#### Key Analytical Takeaways
1. **The "Dual-Ledger" Trap:** The company uses electronic RFID card readers at the main gate/doorway **AND** manual paper sign-in sheets at internal departmental doors.
2. **Manual Reconciliation Bottleneck:** HR is forced to cross-reference doorway swipe data with physical paper books before monthly calculations.
3. **Hardware Vulnerabilities:** Card-swiping systems suffer from "buddy swiping" and lack any real-time connection to statutory payroll deductions.
4. **Automation Readiness:** The organization is already exploring automated/AI projects, confirming enterprise demand for digital workflow replacements.
