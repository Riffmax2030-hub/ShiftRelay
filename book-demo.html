Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Do we need to buy fingerprint scanners or kiosk tablets first?

No hardware is required. Staff clock in on their own smartphones inside your GPS geofence, or staff without smartphones can simply scan their printed Staff ID QR card on a supervisor’s or colleague’s phone with live facial verification.

Is ShiftRelay updated for the Nigeria Tax Act (NTA) 2025 rules?

Yes. Every payroll calculation reflects the NTA 2025 progressive tax bands (effective January 1, 2026), Rent Relief up to ₦500,000/year, PenCom PRA 2014 (8% employee / 10% employer), NHF, NSITF, and bank-ready CSV schedules for GTBank, Access, and Zenith.

Can we test ShiftRelay with a single department or branch first?

Absolutely. Most teams start with one facility or shift cohort during their 30-day pilot, verify attendance accuracy and payslip math on real shifts, and roll out company-wide once HR and Operations sign off.
